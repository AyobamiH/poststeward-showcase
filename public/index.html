Shipping used to make the market go quiet.

The problem was never a lack of things to say. The work itself was full of stories. The problem was that every post pulled the builder out of the build.

[01]

Context switching kills flow

Stop coding. Find an angle. Reconstruct context. Write. Format. Publish. Return to the code and rebuild your mental stack.

[02]

Silence creates a cold launch

If you choose the build instead, the market hears nothing until launch day. Then audience, trust, conversations and pipeline all start at zero.

[03]

The story disappears after the fact

Decisions, failed approaches, breakthroughs and lessons are most useful while they are fresh. Rebuilding that story months later produces generic marketing.

We built PostSteward so development and market-building can happen at the same time.
